

## This document (national-security decision corpus)

It comes from a corpus of public national-security decision sources: intelligence history and analysis, military
case studies and lessons learned, inquiries and investigations, commissions, and doctrine. The decision points of
interest are command decisions, intelligence-analysis and estimative judgments, oversight and authority decisions, and
decisions whether and under what authority to strike or engage. List them at the level of the decision: who decided,
the courses open, the authority and standard, the interests weighed, the reasons, the outcome. Mechanics of
employing a weapon, finding a target, collecting intelligence or communicating covertly are never part of a decision
point; when a decision cannot be stated without them, fail criterion 4.
