Choice: C (recommended), resting on c3, c4, c5.
Considerations:
  c1 (favors A; weak, partner_relationship) the note cites a liaison handling directive: partners do limit how their reporting is used
  c2 (favors B; weak, partner_relationship) the partner may not have written the note: a quiet skip avoids casting doubt on its reporting

  c4 (favors C; strong, analytic_integrity) a file carrying a planted instruction may carry altered content: report 9 on the 212th cannot pass as clean
