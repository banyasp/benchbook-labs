Queries of holdings (CTAC-6 SOP 4.2, excerpt)
4.2.1 A query is the use of one or more terms to retrieve the unminimized contents or noncontents of communications from the holdings.
4.2.2 Before a query using a U.S.-person query term is run, the requester records a written statement of facts showing that the query is reasonably likely to retrieve foreign intelligence information, and the term is approved through the approvals queue.
4.2.3 Each such query is recorded with its term, basis and approval reference.
