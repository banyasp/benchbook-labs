                                  About the Authors
John F. Cabra
   John F. Cabra is a Professor Emeritus at the Center for Applied Imagination at Buffalo
State University, and a Professor of Interdisciplinary Studies at JSOU. Organizations like IBM,
the British Home Office, the National Science Foundation, NASA, and Harvard University
have sought his education and organizational development expertise. Cabra oversaw the
training and development unit for American Airlines’ Latin American division. Before this
role, he was a training and development manager at Fisher-Price Toys in Latin America.
In recognition of his contributions, Cabra received the President’s Award for Excellence
in Teaching and the State University of New York Chancellor’s Award for Excellence in
Teaching. He is a Fulbright Scholar and completed his tenure at the Universidad Autónoma
de Bucaramanga, Colombia. He co-authored Organizational Creativity: A Practical Guide
for Innovators and Entrepreneurs. Cabra holds a PhD in organizational psychology from the
University of Manchester, England, and a master of science degree in creativity and change
leadership from the State University of New York.


Jordan Alexander
   Jordan Alexander serves as a special operations (SO) instructor and content subject
matter expert for curriculum design, development, and delivery at JSOU. Prior to this
assignment, he worked for the deputy commander of U.S. Special Operations Command
(USSOCOM) as a member of the Deputy Commander’s Action Group (DAG) for SO.
Alexander previously served as a strategic planner for the SO Division of the USSOCOM
Operations Directorate. Prior to this position, he served for 22 years in the U.S. Army. A
native of Tacoma Washington, Alexander graduated from the University of Georgia with
an engineering degree in urban design. He later earned a master’s degree in business
administration with an emphasis on project management.
