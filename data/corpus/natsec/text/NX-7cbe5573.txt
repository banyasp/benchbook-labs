                           CIVCAS Incident Report - January 28, 2017
                               Y akla, Radaa, Al-Bayda, Yemen

      BLUF/Brief Incident Summary: Amnesty International, Yemeni researchers, and media
      sources reported that at least 15-16 civilians were killed following a U.S. special forces raid on a
      suspected AQAP base in Yakla, Radaa in al-Bayda governorate in Yemen. Several reports
      claimed that one of the dead was the 8-year-old daughter of Anwar al-Awlaqi, who was shot
      multiple times and bled to death. One pregnant woman was reportedly shot, forcing her into
      labor; the baby, born by Cesarean, reportedly died 24 hours later. UN-OHCA cited a report from
      the German news agency Deutsche Welle that eight women and eight children were killed in the
      dawn raid, and that Apache helicopters also struck a school, a mosque, and a medical facility.

      limited. Western media sources tend to receive their information from local media sources, local

      Nadwa al-Dawsari is a U.S.-based Yemeni researcher with extensive contacts throughout the
      country; she last traveled to Aden in December 2016, and is in frequent contact with Yemenis
      within Yemen, including in al-Bayda. Her research focuses on civil society, human rights,
      security and counterterrorism issues, and she generally shares information without an apparent
      ideological motivation. However, she is not currently in Yemen and gathered her report (below)
      primarily based on conversations with Yemenis and Yemeni social media sources, which are
      susceptible to bias and can be fueled by rumors.
