Conclusion
   Cognitive warfare has the potential to
redefine not only adversarial realities but
also the operational effectiveness of U.S.
SOF. Concepts ranging from cognitive
contagions and personalized influence to
counter-cognitive defenses and SOF-specific
offensive applications underscore a reality:
