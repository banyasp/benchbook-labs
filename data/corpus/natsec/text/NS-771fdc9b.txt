Guide Description
Rather than focusing on the
research topic, the case study method investigates specific
instances by which the topic may be bounded. The case
study approach also enables researchers to describe and
illuminate a phenomenon found in complex social units
with little previous investigation (Merriam, 2009).
Source: U.S. Army graphic by NCO Journal
This research guide is not a comprehensive listing of sources, but is intended to be a starting point from which students can begin their research according to their specific needs.
