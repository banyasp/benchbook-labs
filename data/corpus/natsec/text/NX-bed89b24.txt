  Jeannette Williams retired from the National Security Agency in 1998 after 35 years of service. During
  the 1960s and 1970s, at the height of the arms race, she was an intelligence analyst and reported exten-
  sively on missile and space activities. Later in her career, she served as a senior operational officer and as
  an assistant inspector general. After retiring from federal service, she worked as a contractor at NSA as
  a principal research analyst. Williams holds a degree in English from The Ohio State University and is
  a graduate of the Federal Executive Institute and the National Senior Cryptologic Course.




  Yolande Dickerson began her career at NSA in 1967 in computer operations. After holding numerous
  analytic and administrative positions, she worked at the National Cryptologic School as an education
  and training officer, adjunct faculty member, and member of a career development panel. A member
  of Women in NSA, Dickerson assisted in education and career advancement for women in the Agency.
  Toward the end of her career, she served in the Center for Cryptologic History (CCH) as a historian.
  After retiring from NSA in 1998, she worked under contract as a senior systems analyst in the NSA
  Technology Support Division and later as a senior research analyst at CCH. A graduate of the Univer-
  sity of Baltimore, Dickerson holds a BA degree in sociology and business management.
