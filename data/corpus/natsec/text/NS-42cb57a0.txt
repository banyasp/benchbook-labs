In this handbook, the National Training Center Operations Group examines the art of leadership, its application on the replicated NTC battlefield, and what some of the more successful rotational unit leaders do to build winning teams. Each chapter, written by a senior Observer/Controller/Trainer covers the tactics, techniques and procedures that have been demonstrated to be successful in a specific warfighting function. Leader Development in Contact should prove to be a value addition to every leader’s professional development toolkit.
