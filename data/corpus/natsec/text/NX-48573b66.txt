                 Foreword — Cryptologic Critters

  NSA workers spend their days ferreting out code words; bearing
   up under bureaucratic pressures; squirreling away nuggets of
  data discovered in the vast SIGINT ecosystem; and occasionally
    floundering in the linguistic unknown. We don’t always bat a
thousand, but we have skunked the opposition at times. And while
we are busy as bees working inside these walls, sometimes curious
 critters come to crane their necks at what we do – or hound us on
  to greater efforts! So, sit back and take a gander at some of the
       critters who have wandered into our neck of the woods!
