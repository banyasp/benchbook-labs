Troy C. Troublefield
   Troy C. Troublefield, DBA, PhD is a program manager (MISO) For the United States
European Command, which conducts military information support operations internet
activities. He retired as a psychological operations sergeant major after more than 30
years of service, where he served in multiple senior leadership roles. He holds a BS from
Touro University in Information Technology Management, an MBA with a concentration in
Management of Information Systems from New York Institute of Technology, an MSSI from
the National Intelligence University in Strategic Intelligence, a DBA from the International
School of Management in Internation Business Management, and a PhD from Capital
Technology University in cyberpsychology. He currently serves as an adjunct professor and
dissertation chair for Capital Technology University for PhD students in cyberpsychology,
cyber leadership, and forensic cyberpsychology.
