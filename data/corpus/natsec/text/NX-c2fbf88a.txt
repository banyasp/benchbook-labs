INTRODUCTION:
Letter of Transmittal to Volume 1
REPORTS OF THE INDEPENDENT COUNSEL
The Office of Independent Counsel for Iran/contra Matters is

submitting three volumes of reports to the United States Court of

Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit, Division for the

Purpose of Appointing Independent Counsel:
1.  Volume I: Investigations and Prosecutions
2.  Volume II: Indictments, Plea Agreements, Interim Reports

to the Congress and Administrative Matters
3.  Volume III: Classified Materials
The materials from Independent Counsel's investigations that

are not contained in one of these three volumes have been

deposited with the National Archives. Materials are also held,

some under seal, in the U.S. District Courts for the District of

Columbia, Maryland, and the Eastern District of Virginia and in

the United States Courts of Appeals for the District of Columbia

Circuit and for the Fourth Circuit.
