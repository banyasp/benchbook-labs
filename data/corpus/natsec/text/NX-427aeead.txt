  To close the volume, Professor Erik Dahl of the
Naval Postgraduate School asks, how do we avoid
more domestic intelligence failures? DHS was criti-
cized for not using the appropriate tools and methods
to deal with riots in Portland, Oregon, in 2020; Dahl
argues the backlash may have influenced the inade-
quate response to violence at the Capitol on January 6,
2021. Dahl believes the domestic agencies often fight
the last war: focusing too much on the radical jihadist
threat, which peaked in 2009 (if not earlier), while
ignoring the domestic extremist threat.
