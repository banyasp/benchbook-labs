                              GTA 30-04-001
                                 APR 2019
                 Information Collection Rehearsal (IC RX)
                            Distributed by: Army Training Support Center

When: This occurs before the fires rehearsal and after the mission analysis brief.

Purpose: Validate the information collection (IC) plan, the allocation of IC assets by phase,
and the intelligence architecture by phase. Where possible, the actual asset team leader
should brief the asset’s location, task, and purpose by phase.

Responsibility: Responsibility belongs to the brigade assistant chief of staff for intelligence
(S-2).
