MEMORANDUM FOR Joint Special Operations University (JSOU)

SUBJECT: (U) Academic Year 2025 JSOU Call for Special Operations Papers

1. (U) PURPOSE. As the United States refines national strategy, resourcing levels, and focus
to address the contemporary threat environment, the role of special operations has never
been more vital. In response, USSOCOM is relooking and—in some cases—reimagining its
role in integrated deterrence, crisis response, and counterterrorism operations. Recognizing
the critical need for advanced discourse and creative idea generation, the Joint Special
Operations University Press (JSOU Press) is pleased to announce its call for papers for
Academic Year 2025 (AY25).

2. (U) BACKGROUND. Informed by the 2024 Special Operations Research Topics (SORT),
AY25 papers will focus on critical topics to address the USSOCOM lines of effort PEOPLE,
WIN, TRANSFORM.

a. (U) 2024 SORT. This compilation provides a list of salient topics, grouped by theme,
as a Starting point for those interested in submitting academic papers that are timely,
relevant, and of immediate value to the Special Operations Enterprise (SOE). The questions
included in the SORT were developed with input from academic and operational
organizations representing every enterprise stakeholder and provides a broad array of topics
valued by the SOE.

b. (U) USSOCOM Operational Approach — Destination Postcard. The commander's
approach focuses on three lines of efforts (LOE): PEOPLE, WIN, TRANSFORM. In 2023,
USSOCOM conducted a “Strategic Appreciation” to collectively define and articulate SOF's
value proposition across three critical mission sets for the Department: Integrated
Deterrence, Crisis Response, and Counterterrorism. In 2024, USSOCOM is undergoing a
year of "Analytical Rigor," in which SOF education is one of six identified priorities. JSOU
intends to use SOE academic papers to explore innovative academic initiatives to contribute
to the USSOCOM Operational Approach end state of an “Irreversible Momentum” by 2025.

3. (U) GUIDANCE. As the needs of the SOF enterprise change, the demand for robust
academic discourse rightfully increases. For AY25 academic papers should focus on the
LOE’s below (see Appendix A).

a. (U) PEOPLE LOE. |mprove the execution of Headquarters USSOCOM’s 10 U.S.
Code § 167 service-like responsibilities (Train, Man, Equip, and Educate).

Appendix B: JSOU President Academic Paper Submission Criteria and Awards.
JSOU encourages students, faculty, and SOF practitioners to submit their unique
perspectives that address topics identified in Appendix A in accordance with the
submission guidance below.

1. (U) Submission Criteria.
a. Subject. Must relate to a topic in Appendix A.

b. Length. 1,500-3,000 words (JSOU Quick Look; Occasional Paper).
Additional submission information is located at Publish with JSOU.

2. (U) Categories.

a. CAT 1: JSOU Enlisted Academy focusing on the Career Education Program
(CEP).

b. CAT 2: Professional Military Education (PME) Students and Military
Practitioners.

c. CAT 3: Academic Faculty and Civilian scholars.
3. (U) Timelines.

a. CAT 1. CEP Quarterly Submissions, as directed by the Enlisted Academy
Commandant.

b. CAT 2. In accordance with respective PME intuitions academic paper
timeline. However, to be considered for the JSOU Academic Year Writing Award, PME
papers must be submitted to JSOU PRESS no later than 28 February 2025.

c. CAT 3. JSOU Faculty and Civilian Scholars 1 October 2024 — 28 February
2025.

4. (U) Evaluation.
a. All submissions will be blind peer-reviewed.
b. Suitability for review:
1. Adheres to length, JSOU style, and topic tied to CG's guidance.
2. Must meet established timeline for category submission.

3. Must be unclassified.

3. Once reviewers submit their selections, final reviewers (TBD) have one week to
select a winner.

4. If necessary, the JSOU Provost (CAT 2 & 3) or Command Sergeant Major (CAT
1) serves as a tiebreaker and sends the selected winner to the JSOU President.

5. JSOU President makes the final decision.
5. (U) Incentives and Awards.

a. All winners receive a signed certificate, challenge coin, and personal letter from the

c. CAT 2: The PME winner will be recommended for a Joint Service Achievement
Medal (JSAM) or receive a congratulatory letter/award from USSOCOM.

d. CAT 3: TBD to ensure author applicability (e.g., government civilian, contractor,

e. The JSOU Press will publish winning submission and notable papers.
f. If possible, JSOU will coordinate with USSOCOM to recognize winners (CAT 1, 2, 3)
at SOF Week.

NOTE: For submissions outside of the USSOCOM Headquarters, JSOU will provide authors
