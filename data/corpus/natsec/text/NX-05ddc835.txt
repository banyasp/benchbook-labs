(H) Classiﬁcation and Public Release Review MFR

MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD

SUBJECT: Classiﬁcation and Publication Review of Title of Submission

    1. Purpose. To protect against the accidental or unauthorized disclosure of classiﬁed
       material, policy misstatements, or information detrimental to the United States
       Government (USG), Department of Defense (DoD), or United States Special
       Operations Command (USSOCOM) while promoting an open academic
       environment where timely research and scholarly activities can contribute to the
       profession of arms.

    2. Background. Joint Special Operations University (JSOU) Press operates under the
       principle of “security review at source.” It is the responsibility of the author, when a
       current or former U.S. Government (USG) employee or contractor, to submit
       materials for prepublication review through either their parent organization or the
       DoD prepublication review process.



    3. Check statement(s) that apply.

       _____ I conﬁrm that Title of Submission     underwent classiﬁcation review
       through Organization          on Month/Day/Year     and was approved for
       publication. Documentation attached.

       _____ I conﬁrm that Title of Submission    underwent public release review
       through Organization          on Month/Day/Year      and was approved for
       publication. Documentation attached.

       _____ I conﬁrm that I have never served as an employee of the United States
       Government (USG) or a contractor to the USG. I further state that none of the
       source material used in Title of Submission        is from a source inside the USG
       that could represent an unauthorized breach of conﬁdentiality or appears to be
       proprietary information of a private organization.
