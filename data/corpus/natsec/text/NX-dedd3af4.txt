professional problem: how to
absorb a powerful new instrument
without allowing fascination with
capability to outrun standards,
training, and judgment. That was
true of the computer in Becker’s
day. It is true of AI now. The
decisive question is not whether
the machine is impressive. It is
whether the institution using it is
prepared to govern the change it
brings.
