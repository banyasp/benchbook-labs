In general, the deployment and development of the

Policy governing the training of the PLA has been

conflict than by external crisis. The ideological
conflict is the result of the contradiction between the

maintain that the thoroughly indoctrinated soldier will
always be more important than the weapons he employs. This

who hold these views have been some professional soldiers

lectures." ‘Each side recognizes the value of both weapons
and ideology. “The issue has been one of priorities. By
