                         CHRISTIANE THOMPSON is an assistant
                         professor of interdisciplinary studies at JSOU. She holds
                         a master’s degree in political science from the University
                         of Akron; a master’s degree in military arts from the U.S.
                         Army School of Advanced Military Studies; a Master of
                         Liberal Arts (MLA) degree in extension studies focusing
                         on socio-ecological security from Harvard University,
                         Harvard Extension School, and has completed advanced
                         military education at the U.S. Army Command and
General Staff College. She has over three decades of experience supporting
the U.S. DOD, from hands-on operational engagement to strategic analyst and
planner positions. Her current research interests leverage innovative technologies
such as geospatial information systems and artificial intelligence applications.
