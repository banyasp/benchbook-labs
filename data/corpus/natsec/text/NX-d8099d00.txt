    In looking back on Tom’s career, one might
point to the voice standards that he set or the-
organizations that he chaired as a measure of his
overall contribution. Tom would more likely have
pointed not to his own research accomplish-
ments, but rather to the new generation of speech
researchers that he mentored who still carry on
his work.
