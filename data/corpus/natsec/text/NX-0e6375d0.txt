Contributors to the Final Report
One of the most difficult and important tasks of the Office of Independent Counsel has been
the preparation of this Final Report.  Since this Report seeks to cover the most important aspects
of a lengthy and complex investigation, it is a product of all who have served in the Office of
Independent Counsel in the Iran/contra matter_the lawyers, the investigators, and the support
staff.  To all of them, this office owes a debt of gratitude.
