The US Coast Guard and OSS Maritime
Operations During World War II
LCDR Michael Bennett, USCG


                                           As the Intelligence Community continues its transformation and
                                           the Coast Guard intelligence program experiences enormous
                                           growth, members of both communities would be well-served by
                                           reflecting on the contributions Coast Guard intelligence has made
                                           in the past. From its beginning as the Revenue Marine in 1790, the
                                           Coast Guard’s unique authorities, industry access, and organiza-
                                           tional culture of adaptability have allowed it to make great contri-
                                           butions to intelligence and to important military successes in our
                                           nation’s history.
