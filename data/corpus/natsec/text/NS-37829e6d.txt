Report of the National Commission for the Review of the Research and Development Programs of the United States Intelligence Community
The National Commission for the Review of the Research and Development Programs of the United States Intelligence Community was established pursuant to Public Law 107-306, as amended by Public Law 111-259, to review and report on the status of the research and development programs within the United States Intelligence Community.
