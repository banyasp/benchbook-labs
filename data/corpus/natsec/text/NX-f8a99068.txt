                      Product Definitions




The JSOU Press publishes a variety of academic materials that address United States Special
Operations Command (USSOCOM) priorities and issues of interest to the Special Operations
Forces (SOF) enterprise. All manuscripts should follow the editorial standards as outlined in
the JSOU Press Manuscript Submission Format Guidance and the Chicago Manual of Style,
18th Edition.

Publication Types

   •   Quick look. A quick look is a brief (4–12 pages) overview related to key topics of concern
       and issues of interest to USSOCOM and the SOF enterprise. Subject to double-blind peer
       review.

   •   Occasional paper. An occasional paper is more in-depth than a quick look, though still a
       shorter publication (typically 15–30 pages). Similar to a white paper, an occasional
       paper contains original research or perspective and addresses an academic audience.
       Subject to double-blind peer review.

   •   Monograph. A monograph provides a deep dive into a single topic or aspect of a
       complex topic. It should include new, original, and thoroughly researched information
       that fills a gap in the collective knowledge about the topic. A monograph may have
       more than one author, but it must focus on one subject and be written for an academic
       audience. Subject to double-blind peer review.

   •   Edited volume. An edited volume is a collection of chapters that presents information
       related to a common theme. Each chapter is typically written by a different author and
       should provide original and well-researched insight into a topic that relates to the
       book’s overall theme. Subject to double-blind peer review.

   •   Commentary. A commentary provides analyses, critical insight, or exposure on a specific
       topic important to the SOF enterprise. It may be written by more than one author.
       Commentaries may aim to further research or conversation and should be backed by
       references, examples, and other evidence. Any submission that is primarily opinion in
       nature, addresses a general audience, or does not include new research will be
       considered only as commentary. No peer review required.
