Memory Techniques in the Intelligence
Community
A Tool for Improving Analysis?
Cody Herr
Cody Herr is a senior analyst at US Special Operations Command.


   This article presents the findings of an experiment to test the effect of memory training on
intelligence analysis. The results indicate a significant relationship between memory training and
a boost in recall of key details from intelligence reporting. This strongly suggests a link between
memory optimization and analytic performance. Many organizations require memory testing
and training. In contrast, the Intelligence Community places tremendous demand on intelligence
analysts’ memory, but it does not provide training or testing to improve this skill. Overall, this
article recommends a modest IC investment in memory training to better support policymaking.
