Humans have evolved an exceptional cognitive ability to prioritize structural relationships over surface-level features. Analogical thinking, the
basis of human intelligence and creativity, allows people to map complex patterns from a known scenario to a new, unfamiliar scenario. (The
solution to this example of a nonverbal analogy test is on the following page.)

program of the Office of Intelligence and Analysis of DHS. Before that, he was an analyst and liaison officer for DoD, serving
in various overseas assignments and Washington, DC.
