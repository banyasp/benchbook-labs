                           To think of the human body merely as present in space is to underestimate its operational
                        footprint. Continuously emitting biological signals across multiple sensory domains, the
                        body projects itself into its environment in ways that the adversary can detect, interpret, and
                        exploit. Understanding, managing, and deliberately deploying these persistent emissions can
                        form the foundation of biological operations security (bio-OPSEC).
