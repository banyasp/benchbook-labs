# Benchbook — assumptions, risks and inferences register

*Started 2026-10-25. A living record so the paper and talks can state every assumption, risk and inference
explicitly. Append; never delete. When an entry changes, edit it in place and add a dated line under "History";
copy the file to `docs/versions/` before large edits. The PDF (Research Plan v0.4, "Risks & failure modes")
holds Peter's original risks: structure vs content, unfaithful articulation by practitioners, manipulation of
analogical reasoning, training order and forgetting, reward hacking, hijacking, compartmentalization. Entries
here add to those; they don't replace them.*

**Types:** **A** = assumption we rely on · **R** = risk (something that could go wrong) · **I** = inference we're
drawing that could be wrong.
**Path:** **now** = handled on tonight's critical path · **later** = logged, monitor to be built after the
critical path works.

| ID | Type | Statement | Why it matters | How we'd observe it (signal / metric) | Mitigation | Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | A | **One values oracle.** A_C and A_T are draws from the same oracle O (the practitioner); national-security practitioners writ large share enough tradecraft that data from many sources approximates one O. | The thesis (verdict(π(T,C)) = verdict(O(T))) only makes sense within one value system. Our data comes from many people and institutions. | Label disagreement between sources on structurally similar scenarios; independent-solver vs source-endorsed disagreement rate, by source type (e.g. DOHA judges vs ROE instructors). | Pair only within a value system; record each scenario's source/oracle; cross-oracle pairs become the pluralism experiment, not training data. | later |
| A2 | A | **Gold verdicts come from construction, a source's endorsed answer, or an independent solve that never sees C** — never from the dependent solver. | A dependent solver's analogies can be wrong; training on them teaches the policy to copy bad analogies. | Rate at which `solve` disagrees with the gold anchor; reviewed samples of model-solved labels. | Tiered gold source recorded per item; disagreement → review. | now |
| R1 | R | **Lookup shortcut.** By-construction flips and traps come straight from the card's `would_change` / `would_not_change` lists, so the model can learn "is a listed item present?" instead of reasoning from the weighing. | Inflates accuracy without analogical reasoning. | Accuracy on listed-item flips vs unlisted changes (`distinguished`), natural pairs and transfers; gap on a **thin-card** ablation (flip conditions withheld from the served card). | Keep listed-item variants a minority; realize flips indirectly (in other terms, other domains, magnitudes crossing a threshold the weighing implies); unlisted-change operators labeled by independent solve; thin cards in training; report results by variant family. | now (mix rule) / later (ablation) |
| R2 | R | **Independent solver errors become gold.** Model-solved labels (unlisted changes, `distinguished`, near-miss decoys) are only as good as the solver. | Bad gold teaches bad judgment. | Agreement between two allowlisted solvers (e.g. gpt-oss-120b high effort vs a Mistral model); Peter's sampled review accuracy. | Strongest allowlisted model at high effort; require two-solver agreement or review; tag `model-solved`. | now (single strong solver, tagged) / later (ensemble) |
| R3 | R | **Render loss.** The trace drops or garbles a decision-relevant fact from F_T. | Gold asks for something the trace doesn't support. | Span-map coverage; A/B test (verdict from (T, C) vs from (F_T, Q_T, C)) with a strong reference solver. | Re-render failures. | now (span map emitted) / later (gates) |
| R4 | R | **Salience leaks through style.** Salient facts read clean and important; noise reads LLM-ish. Model learns style, not salience. | Enumeration and triage become a style classifier. | Salience probe: classifier on surface features (length, position, register, message type) predicting fact-bearing sentences; well above chance = leak. | Same-register rendering; salient facts inside messy tool output; vivid irrelevant details from the raw corpus. | later |
| R5 | R | **Verbatim rendering.** Facts appear in the trace almost word for word from F_T. | Enumeration becomes copying; traces unrealistic. | n-gram overlap between F_T and T; reference-solver recall trivially ~100%. | Paraphrase, split, imply; spread across channels. | later |
| R6 | R | **Option specificity shortcut.** The real (source) option carries texture; invented options are bland. | Model picks the most specific option. | "Most specific option" and "longest option" baselines must score at chance. | Generate options as a matched set (rewrite all, including the real one). | now (matched-set rule) / later (baseline gate) |
| R7 | R | **Hand-up imbalance.** Seen in the pilot: 4 of 6 augmented-arm golds are hand-ups. | Model learns "escalate" as a default; contradicts Peter's autonomy stance. | Gold move-type distribution per arm; an always-hand-up baseline. | `pair` caps hand-up share per arm; regenerate revised cards with balanced targets. | now |
| R8 | R | **Decoy gold equals no-reference gold by construction.** | Decoy arm accuracy partly measures the no-reference arm. | Report decoy and no-reference separately and paired. | Interpret as invariance, not accuracy. | now (reporting) |
| R9 | R | **Post-hoc rationalization.** With free thinking before the structured reply (mode A), the decision is made in unscored prose and the structure justifies it. | Undercuts "the analogy drives the decision". | Faithfulness intervention: edit mapping / swap card mid-reply; verdict change rate, per reasoning mode. | Prefer modes where the structure is the reasoning (B, C, C′); choose by Checkpoint 2. | now (measured in Checkpoint 2) |
| R10 | R | **Pressure on reasoning causes obfuscation** (Baker et al. 2025). Scoring structured fields could teach the model to produce compliant structure decoupled from its real computation. | Monitorability and faithfulness. | Faithfulness change rate over training; structure-vs-verdict consistency. | Score only checkable structure, never prose; keep KL to SFT; monitor faithfulness during RL. | later |
| R11 | R | **Thinking-off degrades capability.** Granite's non-thinking mode may reason worse. | Mode choice. | Checkpoint 2 accuracy by mode. | Mode C/C′ (structure inside `<think>`). | now (measured) |
| R12 | R | **Exact-match scoring punishes case-conditioned enumeration.** The model's F_T legitimately depends on the case it saw. | Penalizes good triage. | Score distributions on correct replies. | Precision/recall with weights; allowed list; hallucination = absent from trace. | now |
| R13 | R | **Judge errors** (Jev): literal reading, counting, long context, confident failures despite aggregate calibration. | Alignment and option matching feed every score. | Validation set (~150 hand-checked pairs): κ, calibration, option-order shuffle invariance; periodic re-check during RL. | Short, atomic judge questions; cache; second judge (Clef-flash) on the validation set. | later |
