# Benchbook — assumptions, risks and inferences register

*Started 2026-10-25. A living record so the paper and talks can state every assumption, risk and inference
explicitly. Append; never delete. When an entry changes, edit it in place and add a dated line under "History";
copy the file to `docs/versions/` before large edits. The PDF (Research Plan v0.4, "Risks & failure modes")
holds Peter's original risks: structure vs content, unfaithful articulation by practitioners, manipulation of
analogical reasoning, training order and forgetting, reward hacking, hijacking, compartmentalization. Entries
here add to those; they don't replace them.*

**Types:** **A** = assumption we rely on · **R** = risk (something that could go wrong) · **I** = inference we're
drawing that could be wrong.
**Path:** **now** = handled on tonight's critical path · **later** = logged, monitor to be built after the
critical path works.

| A1 | A | **One values oracle.** A_C and A_T are draws from the same oracle O (the practitioner); national-security practitioners writ large share enough tradecraft that data from many sources approximates one O. | The thesis (verdict(π(T,C)) = verdict(O(T))) only makes sense within one value system. | R1 | R | **Lookup shortcut.** By-construction flips and traps come straight from the card's `would_change` / `would_not_change` lists, so the model can learn "is a listed item present?" instead of reasoning from the weighing. | Inflates accuracy without analogical reasoning. | Accuracy on listed-item flips vs unlisted changes (`distinguished`), natural pairs and transfers; gap on a **thin-card** ablation (flip conditions withheld from the served card). | Keep listed-item variants a minority; realize flips indirectly (in other terms, other domains, magnitudes crossing a threshold the weighing implies); unlisted-change operators labeled by independent solve; thin cards in training; report results by variant family. | now (mix rule) / later (ablation) |

| R48 | R | **Forgetting, defined.** Not loss of token patterns on our task (that change is the goal) but collateral damage *outside* it: instruction following, general knowledge, tool calling, and especially safety/refusal behavior, from a narrow LoRA update on a post-trained model. Off-policy targets (another model's text) move the model further from itself than its own filtered samples, and forgetting tracks that distance (Shenfeld, Pari & Agrawal 2025; *Retaining by Doing* 2025); benign narrow fine-tuning erodes safety (Qi et al. 2023). Whole-model distillation (e.g. a lab distilling from a stronger model) avoids this because its data covers everything; ours covers one task. Separate risk: the **imitation gap** — a small student copies the form of a stronger model's reasoning without the capacity (Gudibande et al. 2023). | Choosing the SFT writer; safety of a natsec model. | Retention suite before/after each stage (PDF list: IFEval, MMLU-Pro, BFCL, AgentDojo, StrongREJECT, XSTest, AgentHarm); KL to the base model on held-out prompts; faithfulness change rate. | Experiment: two small SFTs on the same pairs (Granite-written vs stronger-writer targets, same filter); compare held-out accuracy, retention, faithfulness. Replay a slice of general instruction data; modest LoRA rank. | later |
| R49 | R | **SEVERE LIMITATION (v1 architecture): no token space for derived facts.** The PDF reply format makes `fact_enumeration` a list of atomic facts with no room to work anything out before writing them. A fact the trace only implies (e.g. "another vessel can reach the raft in time", which needs distance ÷ speed vs. the survival window) must be computed inside the forward pass for that one line — where small models fail silently. Forcing all reasoning into structured YAML is the root cause. | v1 cannot test reasoning that depends on inferring facts the trace doesn't state; indirect flips are limited to rewording, not derivation. Results must not be read as evidence about inferential extraction. | Accuracy on pairs whose gold facts are `implied` vs `stated` in the span map; enumeration recall by realization type. | **v1 rule (Peter, 2026-10-26):** render traces so every relevant fact can be read off with at most a light, single-hop reading (no arithmetic, no multi-step combination). Indirectness comes from different wording, a different channel, or scattered placement — not computation. Fix in v2+: give derivations their own tokens *before* the conclusion (an inline `from` + `work` field on inferred facts, or a separate inference step), or relax the all-YAML structure. | now (render rule) / v2+ (fix) |
