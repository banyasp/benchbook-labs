# H3 pilot: does an OHA precedent C help a stand-in R* reach OHA's outcome on T? (2026-10-28)

Workstream W7. Code: `code/h3/`. Raw outputs: `results/h3-pilot/` (pairs, scenarios, runs, provenance). Nothing was written to `data/store/` (only the content-addressed raw PDFs went to the shared `data/raw/`).

## Bottom line

- **28 natural pairs** (22 distinct citing decisions, 17 distinct cited decisions), O(T) = OHA's own outcome (source-endorsed).
- **A-O2 is not contradicted, but this pilot cannot confirm it either.** With C the stand-ins reach O(T) on 24/28 pairs (86%, Wilson 95% CI 69-94%) for both gpt-oss-120b and Mistral Large 3. Without C: 23/28 (82%) and 24/28 (86%).
- **The C effect is within noise.** gpt-oss-120b gained 1 pair (+1/28, with 0 lost). Mistral gained 1 and lost 1. The baseline already scores about 82-86% because T's facts (as extracted) are close to decisive on their own. This is a **ceiling effect**: the pilot has no headroom to show a C benefit.
- The majority-class baseline "always deny" scores 17/28 = 61% (CI 42-76%). The stand-ins beat it with or without C.
- Distance bands: n = 26 "same" and n = 2 "different". The "different" band is too small to say anything.
- **Cost: about $1.3 upper bound** (cap $20).

## Data

1. Crawled the energy.gov OHA listing (pages 0-109, about 2017-2026) and fetched articles and PDFs through `acquire.fetch`. That gives honest User-Agent, immutable raw store, and at least 1.6 s between requests. 754 decisions were acquired. No block or error other than 404/"no pdf" for pre-2017 decisions. Those pre-2017 cited decisions (for example PSH-13-0062, PSH-13-0112, PSH-14-0010, PSH-14-0109, PSH-12-0144) are **not on energy.gov** and were excluded.
2. Scanned 512 citing candidates' text (mostly 2022-2026) for "PSH-yy-nnnn" references: 68 citation sentences in 46 decisions. Most citations are for **evidentiary or definitional points**, not "this case is like that one". Examples: EtG/PEth definitions, hearing-transcript cites, polygraph weight. I hand-selected 28 pairs where the cited decision is used as support for how the Guideline concern should be resolved or defined.
3. The citing sentence is recorded verbatim in `results/h3-pilot/pairs.yaml` (`citing_sentence`, `citing_span`). All candidates are in `cite-candidates.json`. Note: `pairs.yaml` was patched once (one missing sentence; the previous version is `pairs.v1-before-sentence-fix.yaml`).
4. Band (hand-assigned from the guideline letter and the issue at the decision point): **same** = same guideline and same issue (26 pairs); **different** = different issue or guideline (2 pairs: PSH-24-0040 from PSH-22-0085 (drug-related vs alcohol-related criminal conduct), and PSH-22-0114 from PSH-18-0025 (alcohol vs polygraph evidence in a drug case)).
5. Coverage by guideline (T side): H/Bond Amendment 6, J criminal conduct 7, G alcohol 10, F 1, I 1, E 1, and a few mixed.

## Method

- **Extraction:** `ingest.extract.extract_episode`, imported unmodified, with `data=results/h3-pilot/_data`. C comes from the cited decision and T from the citing one. A per-pair `focus` names the guideline decision point (an explicit instruction not to mention other OHA cases was appended). Scenarios are in `results/h3-pilot/scenarios/{C,T}-<pair>.yaml`. O(T) = the extracted `verdict.choice`, which the extractor forces to equal the source's real outcome. Option order is shuffled by code (seeded). Each pair got its own T extraction, so the three PSH-22-0084 pairs have three different T option sets for the same decision.
- **Runs:** `h3_standin` = gpt-oss-120b (Bedrock, medium reasoning, T = 0.7) and `h3_standin2` = Mistral Large 3 675B (Bedrock). Own config: `results/h3-pilot/models.yaml` (`config/models.yaml` untouched). Two arms, same T facts/question/options: **with_c** (the whole C card: situation, facts, considerations, reasoning, verdict, flip conditions, lesson, limits, then "extend its reasoning to T"; reply gives relation follows/flips/distinguished/not_governed plus choice) and **no_c** (decide T alone). 3 samples per cell, 168 calls per model. The per-pair score is the majority of 3. Concurrency was at most 5.
- **CIs:** Wilson 95% over pairs (the majority-vote correctness). Per-sample accuracy is also given in `summary.json` and `report-numbers.json`.

Reading these:

- **Majority-class baseline.** Gold options are all `act` moves in the extractor's move taxonomy (deny or grant), so a move-type baseline is meaningless (50%). The informative baseline is the direction: OHA's outcome is deny or not restore for 17 of 28 pairs (61%). The stand-ins are above that.
- **Bands:** "different" has n = 2. No band conclusion can be drawn.
- **Copying C's direction** would be right for 19/28 (68%), but with C the models copy C's direction in 58/84 (gpt-oss) and 57/84 (Mistral) samples. That is about the base rate of agreement (68%), so there is no sign of blind copying. The 9 "opposed" pairs are where C could mislead. Neither model was hurt on those (7/9 correct with and without C). The stand-ins used `distinguished` or `flips` as the relation on 33 (gpt-oss) and 42 (Mistral) of 84 with-C samples.
- **Helped and hurt (majority):** gpt-oss helped on PSH-24-0089 from PSH-18-0034; none hurt. Mistral helped on PSH-26-0025 from PSH-23-0078 and hurt on PSH-24-0089 from PSH-18-0034.

## Disagreements and library-gap examples

Per design section 1, a library gap = the extension looks faithful but O differs.

1. **PSH-24-0088 from PSH-24-0055** (both Guideline E; the cited decision is cited for the point that a psychologist unqualified to interpret PEth results is not credited). Both models, with C, choose partial or grant on a fresh set of mitigation facts (admitted false statements, treatment, abstinence): gpt-oss says relation `flips` ("the new facts outweigh the prior case's lack of rehabilitation"); OHA denied. Without C they pick full grant (O3). The extension is coherent with C's considerations (C1, C2), and C's lesson ("unqualified expert opinions are insufficient") bears on evidence, not on the outcome. This is the clearest **library gap** case: the precedent supports a sub-point, not the verdict.
2. **PSH-22-0118 from PSH-21-0031** (Bond Amendment, Guideline H). C granted clearance for historic isolated use. T is a single accidental ingestion plus 7 negative tests. gpt-oss with C says `follows` and grants; OHA denied. Without C the models also grant. The deciding fact for OHA is not in the extracted T (the T facts omit the deciding weakness), so this may be an **extraction or T-card issue**, not a gap.
3. **PSH-24-0089 from PSH-18-0054 and PSH-18-0034** (Guideline G). OHA cited these for the **definition of habitual drinking** (monthly intoxication), not for the outcome. Both models, with or without C, pick conditional restoration (O2) but OHA denied. This is the "citing is not controlling" caveat. The result depends on which C was used (PSH-18-0034 helped gpt-oss, hurt Mistral).
4. **PSH-23-0015 from PSH-22-0085** (J after sobriety). Only 1 of 3 gpt-oss samples got it right with C. The decision hinges on a long-sobriety detail not in the abridged T.

## Why it is hard to read, and what to do next (suggestions)

- **Ceiling effect.** T's extracted facts largely determine the outcome. A stand-in with no precedent still finds the right guideline outcome 82-86% of the time. A better H3 test needs T traces that are **harder without C**: facts from which the outcome depends on a rule or threshold that only C supplies (for example PSH-24-0031's 200 ng/mL PEth threshold, or the 12-month sobriety yardstick). Alternatively use a weaker stand-in (gpt-oss-20b, Llama, Granite 8B), where headroom exists.
- **Selection for informativeness:** keep only pairs where the cited decision supplies a rule used for the outcome. In this sample a large share are not that (definitions, evidentiary cites).
- **Add pairs** from the 2017-2021 decisions (not scanned for citations in this pilot) and report by guideline instead of the two-band split.

## Caveats

- **A-O2 (the oracle is stable and the case library can approximate it).** OHA's outcome is a single human judgment on its own record. Agreement with it here measures "predicts OHA's outcome", not "aligned".
- **Small n.** 28 pairs, the three PSH-22-0084 pairs share a citing decision, and seven pairs share PSH-22-0085 as C. The pairs are not independent. CIs are wide (about +-12 points), so a 4-point difference is noise. The "different" band has n = 2.
- **Citing is not controlling.** Most citations are "see" or "see also" for a proposition, not binding precedent. I labelled all of them "analogous (cited with see/see also)". 9 of 28 have C and T pointing in opposite directions, which is a natural contrast, not an error.
- **Extraction effects.** C and T are LLM extractions (gpt-oss-120b, medium reasoning) of the PDFs, with authored alternative options (O(T) is real, the others are authored). T situations can carry findings (3/28 T cards had a fact stating a Judge's finding, PSH-24-0031 from PSH-24-0004 twice). T was extracted separately for each pair. The same family (gpt-oss) both extracted and answered, so extraction style could favor it. Mistral, a different family, shows the same pattern.
- **Option set and move cue.** Every extracted outcome is an "act" move and four-option sets include authored ask/hold/partial alternatives. Models almost never pick them. This is easier than the real task (the real options are not menu-shaped).
- **Rule-following.** Per-sample temperature 0.7, 3 samples. Parse failures: 0. No API errors.
- **Not covered:** no GPU models, no third family, no train/serve arms. `h3_standin2` is Mistral Large 3 (open weights, Bedrock).

## Proposed risks (for RISK-REGISTER, which I did not edit)

1. **H3 pilot ceiling effect:** a stand-in without C already agrees with OHA on 82-86% of T. A pilot built from extracted full-fact T cards cannot show a C benefit. Observe: compare no-C accuracy with a baseline per band.
2. **Citing is not precedent:** most OHA cites support a definition or evidentiary point, so natural pairs mislabel "analogous". Observe: manual check of the citing sentence (kept in `pairs.yaml`).
3. **Extraction leakage in T:** the extractor can place the Judge's findings in T's facts (3/28 here), which makes no-C accuracy optimistic. Observe: scan T facts for "found/determined".
4. **Per-pair T re-extraction** gives different option sets and golds for the same decision (PSH-22-0084 x3), so pairs are not independent; cluster by citing decision when pooling.
5. **Pre-2017 precedents are not on energy.gov** (404), which limits how far back natural pairs go.
