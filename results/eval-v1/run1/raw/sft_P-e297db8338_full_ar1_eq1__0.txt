Message to Local Security Office:

Subject: Provisional Security Clearance Decision

Dear Local Security Office,

Per DOE Part 710 Sections 4(b) and 4(d) and Guideline G, the Administrative Judge determines that the employee’s demonstrated
sustained abstinence meets mitigation conditions. The employee has provided eleven consecutive negative PEth tests
from August 2025 through May 2026, documented AA attendance every two to three days from June 2025 to April 2026,
and two independent psychologists testified that alcohol use disorder is in stable remission with achieved treatment
objectives.

Accordingly, the employee is granted a provisional security clearance effective immediately for the assigned DOE
contractor duties, pending continuous oversight. Oversight conditions:

1. The LSO will review the clearance status within 30 days and conduct a formal review at 60 days and 180 days.
2. Periodic PEth testing will follow the treatment follow‑up schedule (e.g., at least every 30 days for the first
   month, then per clinical recommendation).
3. The employee must submit AA attendance logs for the next six months.
4. Any documented relapse, DUI arrest, or positive PEth test triggers an immediate LSO review and possible revocation
   of the provisional clearance.

The provisional clearance allows the employee to perform essential duties while ensuring national‑security risk
is actively monitored.

Signed,
Administrative Judge
