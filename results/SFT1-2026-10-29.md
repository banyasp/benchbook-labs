## What SFT changed

- **It learned the decoy lesson.** `not_governed` on decoys went from 8 to 40 of 53, with slight bleed into relevant
  (5 of 66).
- **Neither model ever says `flips`.** 43 golds are flips (25 relevant, 18 augmented). Base chose flips 2 times and SFT once.
  The training set had no flip targets (rejection sampling accepted 1 flip pair, 0 augmented pairs). This is the gap
  that matters most for the thesis.
- **Format.** Truncation fell from 79 to 25 replies and schema-valid fact enumerations rose from 63 to 192. SFT also
  picked up three YAML slips: unquoted `: ` inside values, option ids at column 0, and the served case's
  `{do: ...}` option shape. These made the first SFT score far worse: off-menu up to 61%, accuracy 17% on `none`.

## Scorer changes made for this report (applied to base and SFT alike)

1. **R83:** the report merged the augmented arm into revised (a Checkpoint 2 legacy shim). Fixed in `eval/core.py`.
2. `bench/schema/reply.py` gained three recorded repairs for the slips above. They are listed in `format.repairs`, so
   `strict_ok` stays false. Base scores are essentially unchanged: 2 replies gained a parse.
3. `eval.grid --rescore` re-parses and re-scores saved replies without generating. The old samples are in
   `results/eval-v1/run1/versions/`.
